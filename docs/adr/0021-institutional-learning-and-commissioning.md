# ADR: Institutional learning and complete commissioning

## Status

Accepted.

## Decision

Politeia owns a broad institutional model and a narrow trusted kernel. Authorized discovery may establish approved knowledge before a specific operational use is selected. Each reconnaissance grant, task context, and protected effect remains explicitly scoped.

The first delivery closes observation → candidate claims and conflicts → authenticated approval → authorized context, discovery, and governed operations → feedback → proposed corrections → approved changes. Learning cannot authenticate its own evidence, approve its own proposals, or enlarge authority.

The first product acceptance boundary is a complete public-source commissioning package: installation, PostgreSQL persistence, a reusable Rust semantic service, administrative CLI and local authenticated transport, immutable signed generations, verification and atomic activation/rollback, operation after commissioner revocation, and replacement-maintainer recommissioning. Two disjoint synthetic institutions exercise software-development and analytics knowledge without depending on real clients or private commissioner systems.

## Consequences

- Existing public phase and claim IDs remain stable. The context, membership, and correction contracts of Phase 5 are dependencies of the first package rather than deferred learning features.
- Cryptographic authentication resolves trusted installed keys and then checks exact institutional authority. Possession of any registered key does not confer owner, verifier, waiver, or commissioner authority.
- Policy decisions consume actual, exactly bound control evidence. Detector declarations and asserted calibration cannot substitute for a run or independent activation evidence.
- Durable admission, replay/budget reservation, and attempt issuance precede effects. Missing completion after issuance remains unresolved across process restarts and cannot imply safe replay.
- Institutions retain source ownership and immutable evidence. Corrections append provenance-bearing relations; current views and generations derive from admitted truth.
- Broader adapters, transports, deployment topologies, and disconnected delivery remain subject to proven need. The first package does not claim production assurance for those surfaces.

## Supersession

This refines the delivery order in `../19-ROADMAP.md` and the bounded scenario in `../18-FIRST_VERTICAL_SLICE.md`. It removes the requirement to choose one recurring friction before institutional discovery and moves the learning contracts into first-package acceptance. It preserves the constitution, public claim meanings, and historical evidence.
