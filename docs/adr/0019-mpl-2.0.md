# ADR: Mozilla Public License 2.0

## Status
Accepted for starter architecture, subject to normal legal review before commercial release.

## Decision
License the public Politeia codebase under Mozilla Public License 2.0. Consulting terms remain separate from the software license.

## Consequences
- Politeia is open source and current-generation operation does not depend on a commercial license server.
- Distributed modifications to Politeia files remain under MPL-2.0; separate adapters, packs, and client configuration may remain private under the license's terms.
- Repository license metadata, notices, and contribution terms must stay consistent.
- Reversal requires an explicit superseding ADR and migration strategy.
