# First vertical slice

Build one complete commissioning package before adding adapter and deployment breadth. The institutional model may be broad; every grant, context projection, and effect remains explicitly bounded.

## Scenario

In a fresh institution-controlled environment, an institution owner installs the public product and initializes a private workspace with local PostgreSQL and a local service. The owner delegates bounded reconnaissance to a temporary commissioner. A read-only source adapter produces observations across the declared population, including institutional knowledge with no immediate operational use. The commissioner proposes a model and policy/evidence obligations; authenticated owner approval admits exact subjects and provenance. Authorized context and capability discovery derive from the approved model. A signed operational generation is derived, independently verified, and atomically activated. Semantic operations select hard-eligible execution resources, cross the authorized dispatcher, and record durable attempts and outcomes. Feedback produces correction proposals that require approval. Handoff revokes the commissioner and reconnaissance capability while ordinary operation continues; a replacement maintainer recommissions from preserved inputs under a fresh grant.

Run the package for two disjoint synthetic reference institutions: software development and analytics. They exercise the same public machinery and different institutional content. No real client, business obligation, fleet service, or vendor control plane is required.

## Required path

1. client trust domain, institution workspace, owner identity, and expiring commissioner delegation;
2. bounded reconnaissance through one read-only adapter;
3. complete source membership, observation provenance, broad candidate model, contradiction handling, and missed axes;
4. authenticated and authorized owner approval for knowledge and policy/evidence obligations, followed by authority-filtered context and discovery;
5. exact specialization inputs and one reproducible operational generation;
6. one deterministic tool plus one alternate execution resource with evidence-backed capability profiles;
7. hard execution requirements, ordered soft preferences, and an explainable routing decision or escalation;
8. operation specification and preflight;
9. normalized policy decision consuming actual control runs and results and binding the selected resource and routing decision;
10. dispatcher-issued lease and effect port;
11. PostgreSQL state, evidence and transition journals, atomic admission/replay/budget reservation, durable attempts before effects, and a transactional outbox;
12. independent verifier and attestation bound to exact subject + policy + runtime + resource + adapter + delegation;
13. handoff receipt, commissioner/reconnaissance revocation, and operational continuity check;
14. preserved workspace and a reproducible recommissioning check by a replacement maintainer;
15. approved corrections preserving original artifact bytes, reproducible current interpretation, and newly derived context/generations.

## Acceptance

- no frontend bypass exists;
- nested operations re-authorize;
- child delegation is a subset on every axis;
- expired/wrong-audience leases fail;
- attestation cannot be replayed for a different subject;
- contradictions remain visible until approved;
- maintenance identity cannot approve its own authority expansion;
- stale/downgraded protected policy fails closed;
- hard capability, data, locality, budget, and assurance constraints filter before soft routing preferences;
- a verified deterministic operation does not invoke a model merely because one is available;
- the same specialization inputs reproduce the generation identity and bytes, or a declared nondeterminism contract explains the difference;
- commissioner revocation removes reconnaissance and authoring authority without disabling the operational generation;
- no private commissioner or vendor-hosted control plane is required for the bounded proof.
- concurrent admissions admit at most the authorized count, and a daemon restart does not erase replay or budget state;
- interruption after an attempt begins leaves an unresolved outcome that cannot be retried as though no effect occurred;
- install and handoff separate owner, temporary commissioner, operational worker, and independent verifier identities;
- each signed admission resolves a trusted key and checks its authority for the exact institution, workspace, operation, and subject;
- identical generation inputs reproduce exact canonical bytes, and a changed component or invalid signature refuses verification and activation;
- source omission, unsupported included members, fabricated approvals, self-certified independence, foreign workspace data, and stale context receive attributable refusals.
