# Bootstrap and institutional reconnaissance

The first deployment phase is read-only reconnaissance under a bounded, expiring grant.

## Sequence

1. Discover connected systems and topology.
2. Inventory repositories, services, data systems, identities, documentation, workflows, and policy surfaces.
3. Extract observations with exact provenance.
4. Infer candidate authorities and institutional claims.
5. Detect contradictions, stale authorities, duplicated definitions, and missing ownership.
6. Record confidence and `missed_axes`.
7. Present a candidate institutional model.
8. Require human approval for constitutional commitments and unresolved authority choices.
9. Enter shadow mode before consequential enforcement.
10. Calibrate detectors and bindings.
11. Progressively harden approved commitments.

## Epistemic states

`Observation → InferredClaim → ContestedClaim | ApprovedFact`

No inference is silently promoted to approved truth.
