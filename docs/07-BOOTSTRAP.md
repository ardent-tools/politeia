# Bootstrap and institutional reconnaissance

The first deployment phase starts from one institution-owner-approved recurring friction and performs read-only reconnaissance under a bounded, expiring grant inside a client-controlled environment. Only systems and ontology axes inside that grant may be inspected; expanding the scope requires a new explicit approval. The public product initializes a client-owned `InstitutionWorkspace`; private commissioner systems are neither the data plane nor a commissioning dependency.

## Sequence

1. Establish the client trust boundary, institution owner, temporary commissioner delegation, and private workspace.
2. Discover only the connected systems and topology required by the approved friction.
3. Inventory the bounded repositories, services, data systems, identities, documentation, workflows, and policy surfaces named by the reconnaissance grant.
4. Extract observations with exact provenance.
5. Infer candidate authorities and institutional claims.
6. Detect contradictions, stale authorities, duplicated definitions, and missing ownership.
7. Record confidence and `missed_axes`.
8. Present a candidate institutional model.
9. Require institution-owner approval for constitutional commitments and unresolved authority choices.
10. Build and test the minimal institution-specific adapter, pack, policy, and evidence obligation.
11. Enter shadow mode and calibrate detectors and bindings.
12. Derive and verify an immutable operational runtime generation.
13. Hand off the preserved workspace and generation, then revoke or expire commissioner authority without interrupting ordinary operation.

## Epistemic states

`Observation → InferredClaim → ContestedClaim | ApprovedFact`

No inference is silently promoted to approved truth.

Recommissioning deliberately returns to a broader engineering profile under a new explicit delegation. It does not reconstruct deleted source or restore an ambient commissioner.
