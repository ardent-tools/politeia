# Constitution

These are higher-order laws. Lower-level rules and mechanisms must derive from them.

1. **Single semantic authority.** Every authoritative fact has exactly one semantic owner. Institutional authority remains with the institution, not a commissioner, model, harness, or hosted provider.
2. **Mechanical derivation.** Derived representations must be reproducible from declared inputs. Operational narrowing is a non-destructive specialization of canonical source and institution-owned inputs.
3. **Explicit authority.** Every protected externally visible effect requires explicit authority. A role, worker identity, vendor, capability score, or routing decision grants no authority by itself.
4. **Monotonic delegation.** Delegated authority may narrow but never exceed its parent.
5. **Evidence-bearing decisions.** Consequential decisions identify the evidence on which they rely.
6. **Property ≠ detector.** A detector is an evidence-producing mechanism, not the normative proposition itself.
7. **Typed exceptions.** Exceptions are explicit, scoped, authorized, time-bounded where applicable, and auditable.
8. **Exact binding.** Verification and attestations bind to exact artifact, policy, runtime, adapter, and delegation identities.
9. **Explicit failure semantics.** Protected operations define fail-open/fail-closed behavior, recovery, compensation, retry, and idempotency.
10. **Explicit trust boundaries.** Trusted and untrusted actors, adapters, packs, evidence producers, extensions, execution resources, inference sinks, and localities are named. Institution-specific data, secrets, durable state, and production authority remain in the institution's trust domain by default.
11. **Versioned compatibility.** Protocol, schema, pack, and adapter compatibility is explicit and testable.
12. **Provenance by construction.** Generated artifacts, evidence, policy bundles, and runtime generations retain provenance.
13. **Observable completion.** Completion is defined by closure predicates and evidence, not narration.
14. **Independent assurance.** Where independence is required, the actor cannot certify itself.
15. **Self-maintenance without self-authorization.** The system may derive, repair, reconcile, and safely strengthen approved commitments but may not grant itself new authority or weaken protected commitments.
16. **Semantic compression.** Prefer fewer independent truths that derive many concrete projections.
17. **Honest enforcement.** Use the strongest semantically faithful enforcement surface, not the strongest-looking surface. Structure sets the correctness floor; worker intelligence may raise capability but may not replace an available structural guarantee.
18. **Uncertainty is data.** Inferred institutional knowledge retains confidence, conflict, provenance, and missing axes until approved.
19. **Self-sufficient commissioning.** The public product distribution contains the machinery needed to commission, specialize, verify, hand off, and recommission Politeia in an institution-controlled environment without private commissioner infrastructure.
