# Product charter

## Purpose

Provide a harness-independent institutional control plane for human and machine work.

The control plane makes the organization's operating model explicit enough that machine workers can discover what matters, obtain only the authority they need, act through mediated effects, provide evidence for consequential transitions, and improve the institution's machine-readable projections without silently changing its constitutional commitments.

Politeia enters through institutional friction: repeated corrections, uncertain ownership, incompatible procedures, costly coordination, and work whose correctness depends too heavily on individual judgment. It turns that friction into provenance-bearing observations, approved structure, and enforceable contracts.

## Success criteria

The product is successful when:

1. a new organization can be commissioned from the public distribution in its own environment, beginning with bounded read-only reconnaissance;
2. observed facts retain provenance and uncertainty;
3. conflicting authorities are surfaced rather than guessed away;
4. approved commitments compile into agent context, policy, verification obligations, and enforcement bindings;
5. protected effects are completely mediated;
6. delegated authority is monotonically narrower than parent authority across every declared axis;
7. consequential transitions are evidence-bearing and exactly bound;
8. agents can operate through multiple harnesses without semantic divergence;
9. recurring human corrections can become durable system improvements;
10. autonomous maintenance cannot expand its own authority;
11. the system's conceptual kernel remains smaller than its integration surface;
12. the public source distribution is sufficient to commission, specialize, verify, and hand off a deployment in a client-controlled environment;
13. client-specific truth, credentials, durable state, evidence, and production authority remain client-owned by default;
14. a replacement maintainer can recommission from preserved source and workspace after the temporary commissioner is revoked;
15. operation of the current generation does not require an original commissioner, model vendor, harness vendor, or hosted product control plane.

## Product-level invariant

The product may own the meaning and governance of work without owning every system where work physically occurs. Commissioning authority is temporary; institutional sovereignty is not.
